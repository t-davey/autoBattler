# Lore bible

What is true in the world. Status labels are defined in [the lore guide](README.md#status). **Spoilers throughout.**

## What the player is told

**Canon.** The premise in [World](../design.md#world) is all the player knows at the start.

## The core truth

**Canon: the Rendering.** Oil is rendered from the dead.

- The Ember Works weren't only foundries. They are where the Undercroft's dead were sent to be burned for light.
- The dead are restless because the Lamps have never let them rest.
- Those who chose the dark refused to be burned, or to burn anyone else.
- The dark is the shadow the Lamps cast, and Guardians are old Entities who put the Lamps out on purpose.

**Canon:** the player is complicit. The Oil in each Hero's lantern is the same Oil, and it is on screen in every Battle.

## The reveal

**Canon.** It lands at the end of Act 3 in the first Difficulty Tier, through a ledger Item, so that the replay carries new meaning.

## The Acts

**Canon**, beyond what the core truth states. Each Act's place is in [World](../design.md#world), where the names stay provisional.

| Act | Its part in the Rendering |
|---|---|
| 1. The Undercroft | Where the dead were kept before being sent on, and where they don't lie still |
| 2. The Ember Works | Where the dead were burned. The burned workers and slag-creatures are what the trade left behind |
| 3. The Lightless Court | Home of those who chose the dark |

## The Mouldering Warden

**Provisional.** The first Guardian pleads with the Party. It reads as a monster's threat on the first visit, and as a plea once the player knows the truth. It asks the Party to leave its Lamp dark so the dead can rest. The line is in [the Act 1 Fragments](act-1-fragments.md).

## Rules as story

Each rule keeps working as designed. These are its in-world reasons.

| Rule | In-world reason | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A Wipe costs only time, and the Party returns to Town | The Last Lamp won't release the Heroes either | Provisional |
| Guardians respawn | They are still at their posts | Provisional |
| Lamps go dark at each new Difficulty Tier, yet stay lit in earlier ones | None yet. "They've used up what they were fed" doesn't explain Lamps staying lit, and edges towards light as a resource | Open |
| Oil regenerates during a Run and refills fully in Town | None yet | Open |
| Each Party relights Lamps for itself | None yet | Open |
| Fresh Parties share the Stash | None yet | Open |

## Open questions

- **The ledger:** an Item is gear a Hero can equip, so how does a ledger fit? Plan this with the real UI, when that gets built.
- **Who knows:** does Town know where its Oil comes from? Does anyone still run the Rendering, and if so, who fills the Heroes' lanterns?
- **The Classes:** what are the Lamplighters and Sin-Eaters in this history? Each sounds like it could be an office of the trade.
- **The Court:** who rules it, and what do they want from a Party that keeps relighting Lamps?
- **The ending:** what follows the reveal? The design has the Party keep going, so the story needs a reason, or a reason not to stop.
- **After the reveal:** do any Hero lines or items acknowledge it once it has happened?
