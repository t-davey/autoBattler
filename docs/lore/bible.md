# Lore bible

What is true in the world. Status labels are defined in [the lore guide](README.md#status). **Spoilers throughout.**

## What the player is told

**Canon.** One town survives around the Last Lamp, which holds back a rising dark. Parties descend to relight old Lamps in the depths. The tone is grim gothic.

## The core truth

**Canon: the Rendering.** Lamp Oil is rendered from the dead.

- The Ember Works weren't only foundries. They are where the Undercroft's dead were sent to be burned for light.
- The dead are restless because the Lamps have never let them rest.
- The dark isn't an invader. It is simply where the dead would otherwise lie.
- Those who chose the dark refused to be burned, or to burn anyone else.

The player is complicit: the Oil in each Hero's lantern is the same Oil, and it is on screen in every Battle.

## The Acts

**Provisional**, beyond what the core truth states. Act names come from [docs/design.md](../design.md#world).

| Act | Place | Its part in the Rendering |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Undercroft | Flooded crypts beneath Town | Where the dead were kept before being sent on, and where they don't lie still |
| 2. The Ember Works | Abandoned foundries that once fed the Lamps | Where the dead were burned. The burned workers and slag-creatures are what the trade left behind |
| 3. The Lightless Court | A frozen palace | Home of those who chose the dark |

## Rules as story

**Provisional.** Each rule keeps working as designed. These are its in-world reasons.

| Rule | In-world reason |
|---|---|
| Lamps go dark at each new Difficulty Tier | They've used up what they were fed, so each relight demands something richer |
| A Wipe costs nothing and the Party returns to Town | The Last Lamp won't release the Heroes either |
| Guardians respawn | They are still at their posts, in a trade that never stopped |
| No consumable restores Oil, and it refills fully in Town | Only Town has it, and Town doesn't say where it comes from |
| Each Party relights Lamps for itself | **Open:** no reason yet, and this is the weakest fit |
| Fresh Parties share the Stash | **Open:** no reason yet |

## The Mouldering Warden

**Provisional.** The first Guardian's line: "Leave it dark. Let us sleep."

It reads as a monster's threat on the first visit, and as a plea once the player knows the truth.

## Open questions

- **Where the reveal lands:** at the end of Act 3 in the first Difficulty Tier, or at the reset into the second, so that the replay carries new meaning?
- **How it lands:** a Boss line, a ledger item, or an Act introduction?
- **Who knows:** does Town know where its Oil comes from? Does anyone still run the Rendering, and if so, who fills the Heroes' lanterns?
- **The Classes:** what are the Lamplighters and Sin-Eaters in this history? Each sounds like it could be an office of the trade.
- **The Court:** who rules it, and what do they want from a Party that keeps relighting Lamps?
- **The ending:** what follows the reveal? The design has the Party keep going, so the story needs a reason, or a reason not to stop.
- **Mechanics:** do any Hero's lines or items acknowledge the reveal once it has happened?

## Set aside

Considered as the core truth and not chosen. Their lines and ideas can be reused where they fit.

- **The Long Shadow:** the dark is the shadow the Lamps cast, and Guardians are old keepers who put the Lamps out on purpose. Spare line, an item: "A keeper's snuffer, worn smooth with use. It was never meant for trimming wicks."
- **The Remembered Town:** the town died long ago and survives only in the Last Lamp's memory, which is why Town is a menu. "Dead all along" is a familiar twist. Spare line, Away Report: "Returned to Town. The baker waved. He has waved exactly that way every time."
