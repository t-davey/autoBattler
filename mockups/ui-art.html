Riveted iron, bevelled and grimed, with cast brass brackets at the corners. Rivets space themselves to the plate's size.

The nameplate hangs between two lanterns on wrought brackets. Their flames flicker and throw a dithered halo, the way the scene lights a Room.

One stain: a thin seep of oil runs down the inner edge from a rivet. Grime on first reading, and later the thing itself, leaking through the Council's own fittings.

Gauges are lamp glass between cast caps, brass for Oil and iron for health, marked in tenths like a measure, with a bright meniscus and the odd bubble.

A clerk's tally in pencil at the foot, each group struck through. On a first visit it's a clerk counting something. Once the player has opened a chest and read "a tally of names, each struck through", it's the same hand.

Tickets, like a ration or a ferry, with a stub, a string hole, notches at the perforation, and a printed red rule. The stub carries the kind's mark: a flame for a Boon, stacked tins for Stacking, and for Wall prep a cog against the automata or a vial against poison. The taken ticket is punched through with a flame-shaped clipper, so you can see the page behind it.
